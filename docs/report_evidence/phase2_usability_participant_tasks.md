# Phase 2 formative usability participant materials

## Information and consent

You are invited to take part in a short formative exercise about a catalogue web interface. The session should take approximately 15–20 minutes. We are evaluating the interface, not you, and there are no right or wrong ways to explore it.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline any question or stop at any time without giving a reason. If you stop after the tasks have begun, you may request that the anonymous observations already recorded during the session not be used; the moderator will ask only whether those observations may be retained. No sensitive information is requested. We do not collect your name, contact details or employer. No audio, video or screen recording occurs by default, and no recording is planned for this exercise. Consent is recorded only as confirmed or not confirmed. This small formative exercise is not presented as having formal ethical approval.

During each task, please say aloud what you are looking for, expecting or finding confusing. The moderator may repeat the task but will not explain the controls. You will be asked one ease question after each task.
